# M5.1 — Variable & Control Isolator + Safety & IRB checklist

## Scope and decisions

- `/variables?ideaId=…`: mobile-first React/Tailwind card canvas, linked from saved Idea, Evaluation and the shell.
- Science: IV, DV, controlled conditions, control/comparison groups, quantitative unit and repeats per group.
- Computing: input, output, baseline, metric, data split, quantitative unit and independent test rounds.
- Learner-authored answers only; switching presets does not generate answers and retains both in-page drafts. Only the selected preset is persisted.
- Additive optional `Idea.isolator` / `Idea.safetyChecklist` schema fields preserve older Guest Ideas. Incomplete designs can be saved. Persistence failures show an error and retain the dirty draft.
- Rigor checks comparison presence, quantitative unit and integer n ≥ 3. Suggested units are not exhaustive: custom units require an explicit learner confirmation that they are quantitative; this is a self-report, not scientific validation. Empty units do not pass even with confirmation.
- Evaluation uses a deterministic **app heuristic**, not official competition scoring: 0/1/2/3 completed checks → 1/3/5/7. Missing IV/DV/controls or input/output/metric/data split caps rigor at 5. Existing Ideas without an isolator keep their prior fallback behavior. Saved isolator evidence overrides AI-proposed rigor; the weighted overall result is recomputed.
- Existing Evaluation snapshots do not silently change when designs change; UI asks learners to re-evaluate within quota. Reopening Evaluation selects the newest saved result.
- New Proposal assembly prefers the saved isolator over old Variables Lite copies. Computing input/output populate independent/dependent fields; controlled fields are not invented. Already-edited Proposal content is preserved, not overwritten by isolator changes.
- Safety screening includes learner-written isolator fields. Checklist families and supervision guidance are grounded in **RESEARCH.md §8.2 and §10**: human Informed Consent, animal permission, BSL-1/BSL-2 lab review, chemical supervision and high-voltage/radiation supervision, with shared advisor and IRB/SRC requirement review.
- RESEARCH.md does not specify numbered forms or official form URLs. No new form numbers, official URLs, approval claims, BSL assignment or procedural lab/chemical instructions were invented. The pre-existing Safety Screener advisories are unchanged.
- Checklist ticks mean the learner reviewed a preparation item, **not** that a teacher/IRB/SRC approved it. No names, signatures, participant details or document uploads. All checklist items are non-blocking; Proposal remains accessible with active flags.

## Limits / release status

- Checklist is preparation guidance, not legal/safety advice or official approval. Teacher/organizer verification of the current competition year remains necessary.
- Keyword screening retains its documented false-positive/false-negative limitations; absence of flags is not a safety clearance.
- A saved custom-unit confirmation does not establish measurement validity; a teacher must review the design and scale.
- No live AI, external school/IRB workflow, login, cloud synchronization, deployment, push or official competition compliance validation was performed.
- Upstream dependency audit findings were not remediated in this card; no release-security claim.
- The downstream M5.2 implementation card and scheduled independent final review remain separate phases.
